# Theoretical Background

## The Geometric Standard Model (GSM)

The Geometric Standard Model proposes that fundamental physics constants emerge from the projection of the E₈ exceptional Lie algebra onto the H₄ icosahedral Coxeter group, where the golden ratio φ = (1+√5)/2 governs all perturbative corrections.

## E₈ Lie Algebra

### Properties
- Dimension: 248
- Rank: 8
- Coxeter number: h = 30
- Coxeter exponents: {1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29}
- Casimir degrees: {2, 8, 12, 14, 18, 20, 24, 30}

### Root System
E₈ has 240 roots divided into:
- 112 integer-coordinate roots: (±1, ±1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0) permutations
- 128 half-integer roots: (½, ½, ½, ½, ½, ½, ½, ½) with even sign changes

## H₄ Coxeter Group

### Properties
- Order: |H₄| = 14400 = 120 × 120
- Rank: 4
- Coxeter exponents: {1, 11, 19, 29}
- Casimir degrees: {2, 12, 20, 30}
- Eigenvalue: φ (golden ratio)

### Connection to Golden Ratio
H₄ is the symmetry group of the 120-cell and 600-cell regular polytopes. Its characteristic polynomial has golden ratio eigenvalues.

## The T-Operator Derivation

### Step 1: Octonion Connection
The imaginary octonions Im(𝕆) have dimension 7. This gives the numerator of T.

### Step 2: Casimir Assignment
Each physical constant is associated with an E₈ Casimir invariant based on its physics sector (gauge, mass, mixing, cosmology).

### Step 3: Gauge Embedding
The Standard Model gauge group embeds in E₈:
```
G_SM = SU(3)₂ × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y ⊂ E₆ ⊂ E₈
```

### Step 4: CP Classification
Each constant transforms under CP with eigenvalue ±1, determining the sign.

## Summary

The T-operator encodes:
- **7**: Octonion structure
- **k**: Gauge embedding
- **n**: E₈ Casimirs
- **φ**: H₄ eigenvalue
- **±**: CP transformation

All components derive from geometry, giving zero free parameters.
